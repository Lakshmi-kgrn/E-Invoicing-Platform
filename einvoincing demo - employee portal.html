Why E-Invoicing?
Four reasons every UAE business must act now
🏛️
FTA Regulatory Mandate
The Federal Tax Authority requires all eligible UAE businesses to adopt the PINT AE standard. Non-compliance risks penalties and audit exposure.
⚡
Real-Time Validation
Invoices are validated and submitted to the FTA (C5) in real time, eliminating manual errors and reducing rejection rates to near zero.
🔗
Seamless ERP Integration
Native integration via API or file upload with your existing ERP — SAP, Oracle, Zoho, or any other — with zero disruption to your workflow.
📊
Full Audit Trail
Every invoice carries a complete lifecycle record — submission, acknowledgment, buyer delivery, and status response — all in one dashboard.
